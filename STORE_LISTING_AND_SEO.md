## 3. Full Store Listing Description (Copy & Paste Ready)

Take your MyAEW viewing experience to the next level. **MyAEW Plus** transforms the awkward, cluttered default video player into a modern, cinematic streaming experience with native playback speed controls.

### 🌟 Why You Need This Extension
The default MyAEW layout suffers from awkward black voids, rigid containers, and lacks essential playback controls. Watching AEW Dynamite, Collision, or Rampage shouldn't feel clunky. MyAEW Plus fixes the layout, centers the stream, adds an expansive Theater Mode, and gives you total precision control over playback speed.

---

### ⚡ Key Features

#### 🎬 1. Modern Player UI & Layout Fixer
- **Cinematic Theater Mode (Hotkey 'T'):** Expand the video to fill your widescreen monitor cleanly without having to enter OS-level full screen.
- **Void & Padding Removal:** Eliminates awkward empty black bars, padding misalignments, and strange container bounds.
- **Centered, Responsive Player:** Perfectly fits modern 16:9 displays and automatically scales with your browser window.
- **On-Screen HUD:** Get instant visual toasts when switching speeds or toggling theater view.

#### ⏩ 2. Precision Playback Speed Controller
- **Custom Speeds (0.25x to 4.0x):** Fine-tune playback in 0.05x increments using intuitive sliders or quick-click presets (1.0x, 1.25x, 1.5x, 2.0x, 3.0x, 4.0x).
- **Persistent Speed Memory:** Your selected playback speed automatically saves across episodes and reloads.
- **Smart Auto-Speed (Buffer Throttling):** Automatically adjusts playback speed dynamically based on live buffer telemetry to eliminate buffering and stutter.
- **Finish By Target Time Mode:** Tell the extension what time you need to finish watching (e.g. 10:30 PM) and it calculates and adjusts the exact playback rate required to finish on schedule!

#### 🚀 3. Bitmovin Buffer Booster
- Automatically reconfigures the underlying Bitmovin video player to buffer ahead up to 120 seconds of audio and video, preventing stream freezes during high-traffic AEW live shows.

#### ⌨️ 4. Intuitive Keyboard Shortcuts
- **`T`** - Toggle Theater Mode (Expands player to full browser width)
- **`]`** - Increase speed by +0.1x
- **`[`** - Decrease speed by -0.1x
- **`R`** or **`\`** - Reset speed to 1.0x (Normal)
- **`S`** - Open / Close Speed Controller menu
- **`Esc`** - Close popup
